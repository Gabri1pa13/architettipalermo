Why Choosing a Local Architect in Palermo is Crucial

Palermo's architectural landscape is a unique blend of history, from Arab-Norman heritages to Baroque and Liberty styles. A local architect doesn't just design; they navigate the local bureaucracy (Soprintendenza, Comune) and understand the specific climate of Sicily.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an architect cost in Palermo?

Architect fees typically range from 5% to 12% of the construction value, depending on the complexity and scope of the project.

Do architects in Palermo speak English?
