Renovating Property in Palermo: A Comprehensive Guide for Expats

Buying and renovating a house in Sicily is an exciting journey, but it requires expert local guidance to navigate regulations and ensure quality.

Renovation Costs in Palermo 2025

On average, a full high-end renovation in Palermo costs between €800 and €1,500 per square meter. This includes materials, labor, and professional fees. Historic center properties may require additional budget for restoration-specific techniques.

The Renovation Process

  1. Due Diligence: Checking the legal and technical status of the property.
  2. Design & Planning: Architectural project and 3D visualizations.
  3. Permits: Obtaining CILA or SCIA from the Palermo Municipality.
  4. Project Management: Supervising the construction and ensuring deadlines are met.
